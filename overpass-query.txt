Vienna Hide & Seek v3.3.1 — Overpass fallback queries

Core districts, stations and U-/S-Bahn lines no longer use Overpass. They use Stadt Wien WFS.
Museums, parks, libraries and hospitals also use Stadt Wien WFS.

For the remaining OSM-only Tentacle categories, use a fixed Vienna bounding box
(south, west, north, east): 48.117668,16.18218,48.322571,16.577511

Cinema:
[out:json][timeout:30];
nwr["amenity"="cinema"](48.117668,16.18218,48.322571,16.577511);
out center tags;

Zoo:
[out:json][timeout:30];
nwr["tourism"="zoo"](48.117668,16.18218,48.322571,16.577511);
out center tags;

Aquarium:
[out:json][timeout:30];
nwr["tourism"="aquarium"](48.117668,16.18218,48.322571,16.577511);
out center tags;

Amusement park:
[out:json][timeout:30];
nwr["tourism"="theme_park"](48.117668,16.18218,48.322571,16.577511);
out center tags;

The bounding-box form avoids the extra Overpass area lookup used in older builds.
